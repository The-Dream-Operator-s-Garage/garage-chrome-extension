pathos.cloud :: history upload — the pages you visit, on your own pathchain.

Mint it on pathos.cloud: the identity window → Keys → New key → Chrome history. It acts as your Chrome alter-ego, may only add visits to its own history and pin pages onto your pins (the window's Pin button), and cannot publish anything. It is kept on this computer only. A key made before 0.2.0 pins once you switch on Pins beside it in the Keys list.
